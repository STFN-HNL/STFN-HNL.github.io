Privacy policy

BrainHub is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, BrainHub reads Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets data from the owner's account, plus creating calendar events. Access is granted through Google OAuth and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How data is handled

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own use.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data from other people's Google accounts is collected.

BrainHub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
